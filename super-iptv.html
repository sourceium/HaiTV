About streams

Super IPTV is a player only — it hosts no channels. The iptv-org catalog lists publicly available streams that broadcasters or third parties provide; many go offline, are geo-blocked, or block browser (CORS) access, so some channels simply won't play. When a channel has backup streams, the player tries them automatically.
